Projector workshop service

Projector repair is carried out at our workshop — we do not offer on-site call-outs for projectors. Send the unit in for diagnosis and repair; we quote accurately and only fit parts that are required, with clear advice on whether repair or replacement is the better option.

  • Optical and lamp-related issues
  • Business and education fleets
  • Advice on repair versus replace
