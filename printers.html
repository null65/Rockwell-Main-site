What we cover

Epson large format printers.

We specialise in Epson large format printers across the UK. Our engineers are Epson trained and attend site with the parts required to get you up and running quickly. All work is quoted accurately. Along with repairs we install and deinstall equipment and can offer operator training on software and hardware.

We handle both in and out of warranty repairs and preventative maintenance. We always attempt to fix issues over the phone first so you only need on-site support when it is really required. All repairs come with a warranty. If printheads are required we use the manufacturer’s fixed-price repair option.

Partnership

Epson trained. Warranty work.

Our engineers are Epson trained and qualified to undertake warranty work on behalf of Epson. When you call, you speak to an engineer who knows the product rather than a call centre reading from a script.

Before we visit

To get the best from your service visit.

Ink & media

Please ensure you have sufficient ink (at least 50%) or replacement cartridges. We carry test media; if you have issues with a specific paper stock we will need enough to carry out tests.

Service codes

Please let us know all service codes and error numbers, and any unusual cleans, scuff marks or print results — that detail helps both of us reach a fix quickly.

Repair only

We do not sell equipment. We will always give you the most economic route and only fit parts that are required.
