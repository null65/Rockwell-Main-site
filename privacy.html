How we handle personal data when you use this website or contact us.

What this site does today

As of the date above, this is a brochure-style website. It does not run advertising trackers or web analytics that identify visitors, and it does not set marketing cookies.

Information you send us

If you use the contact form, we collect the details you provide: name, email address, phone number (optional), subject, and message. That information is sent to our form handler so we can reply about printer or projector service.

If you email or phone us, we process whatever you include so we can handle your enquiry.

Technical delivery

  • Contact form: submissions are posted to a form endpoint operated for this site (amt.driverjack.co.uk) so messages can be delivered to us.
  • Fonts: page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser may request font files from Google’s servers when you view the site.
  • Hosting / server logs: whichever host serves the site may keep standard server logs (for example IP address, browser type, pages requested, and time). Those logs are used for security and reliability, not for marketing profiles.

Cookies and similar storage

When the live cookie banner is active, your choice (accept or essential only) is stored in your browser’s localStorage under the key rockwell_cookie_consent so we do not keep asking. That preference stays on your device; it is not sent to us as a tracking cookie.

We do not currently set analytics or advertising cookies. If we add optional analytics later, they will only load after you Accept, and this policy will be updated to name the tool and what it records.

Why we use personal data

  • To respond to service and sales enquiries
  • To arrange engineer visits, quotes, and follow-up
  • To keep the website secure and working
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply

Our lawful bases are typically legitimate interests (running a B2B service business and securing the site) and/or steps prior to a contract when you ask us to quote or book work. Where consent is required (for example optional analytics cookies), we will ask first.

How long we keep data

Contact enquiries are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and any related service history, then deleted or archived according to normal business practice. Server logs are retained only as long as the host needs for security and operations.

Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We may share what is necessary with:

  • People at Rockwell Hitec / Inkjet Service who need it to respond
  • Technical providers that help run the website or deliver form messages
  • Epson or other partners only when that is needed for warranty or service work you have asked about
  • Professional advisers or authorities if the law requires it

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Depending on the situation, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. To exercise rights relating to this website, email us using the addresses above.

Children

This site is aimed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

When we add analytics, change hosting, or alter how forms work, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date so it stays accurate.
