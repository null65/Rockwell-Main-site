Who we are

Part of Rockwell Hitec since day one.

Inkjet Service has supported Epson large format customers since 2000, as part of Rockwell Hitec Ltd (est. 1982). We work with resellers and manufacturers across Epson and Fujifilm fleets — and we aim for industry-leading first-fix rates.

Engineers attend site with the parts needed to get you running quickly. We quote accurately, fit only what is required, and use manufacturer fixed-price options when printheads need work.

We’ll always try to resolve issues over the phone first, so you only need an on-site visit when it’s really required. All repairs come with a warranty.

Working with us

Help us help you faster.

For the best visit, have at least 50% ink available, note any service codes or error numbers, and tell us about unusual cleans, marks or print results — that detail helps us fix it faster.
