Who we are

Two names. One company.

Inkjet Service is part of Rockwell Hitec Ltd, which was established in 1982. Rockwell Hitec have manufactured products in the photographic, motion picture and imaging industries for over thirty years.

Inkjet Service Limited was formed in 2000 and has become a well-known printer repair service company in the UK, predominantly working with Epson and Fujifilm. Dedicated engineers are located nationwide to offer a next-day breakdown service.

We work with resellers and are trusted by manufacturers with high-profile products for in and out of warranty repairs. We do not sell equipment — only repair — so you can rely on trusted advice about when to repair or replace. Projector repairs are workshop-based; we do not offer on-site call-outs for projectors.

Working with us

To get the best from your service visit.

Please ensure you have sufficient ink (at least 50%) or replacement cartridges. We carry test media; if you have issues with a specific paper stock we will need enough to carry out tests. Let us know all service codes and error numbers, and any unusual cleans, scuff marks or print results — that detail helps both of us reach a fix quickly.

If you reach voicemail, leave a contact number. The message is emailed to our engineers so you get the fastest call-back.
