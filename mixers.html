Overview

HV3 and MM3 mixers.

Founded in 1982, Rockwell Hitec manufactures automatic chemical mixing devices predominantly for the photographic and motion picture industries. We offer two main products — the HV3 and MM3 — which can be adapted to mix liquids to a high level of accuracy.

Rockwell have manufactured and installed more than 1,000 machines worldwide, and are in our fourth generation of product, with electronics and mechanics designed and built in the UK. Custom products can be made to suit lab sizes from a few litres per day to thousands of litres per hour. Spare parts are manufactured and sold from our factory in the UK.

  • Simpler and more failsafe than hand mixing
  • Uniform, accurate mixing for consistent quality
  • Reduced chemical costs using large chemical packs
  • Easier health and safety compliance; less employee exposure to chemicals
  • Fewer mistakes and potential spills
  • Chemical handling and pumping equipment also available — call with requirements

HV3

Up to 300 litres per solution per hour. Modular design — up to 7 solutions per cabinet, each with up to 5 concentrates/overflow and water.

MM3

Up to 40 litres per solution per hour. Same modular approach, suited to smaller consumption while remaining configurable for the lab.

Suitable for users from around 10 litres to tens of thousands of litres per day. Failsafe operation mixes correctly or stops and warns the operator for safe unattended use. Remote monitoring from a PC allows configuration changes and stoppages.
